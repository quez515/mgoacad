Step 2 — The Options Questionnaire Answer Like This
Every broker asks similar questions during options approval. Here are the questions with the exact answers that get you approved for Level 2.
Q1
What is your investment objective?
They want to see that you understand options carry risk and you are using them with purpose — not gambling.
✓ SELECT
"Growth" — signals you understand risk and are investing with a goal, not speculating recklessly.
Q2
What is your options trading experience?
Answering "none" can get you denied. If you have studied options — including through MGO Academy — that counts as education and experience.
✓ SELECT
"Limited" — if asked for years, select 1–2. You have studied options concepts. That is limited experience, not zero experience.
Q3
How many options trades do you plan to make per year?
They want to see that you are serious about learning, not placing one random trade. Pick a number that shows active but measured participation.
✓ SELECT
"10–20 trades per year" — shows you are engaged and learning, not gambling or inactive.
Q4
What is your annual income and net worth?
Answer this truthfully. Brokers use this to assess whether you can absorb potential losses. You do not need to be wealthy to get Level 2 approved.
✓ BE HONEST
Answer accurately. Most brokers approve Level 2 at any income if you answer the other questions correctly. Do not inflate your numbers.
Q5
What level of options approval are you applying for?
This is the most important question. Level 2 is exactly what you need — it covers buying calls and puts, which is everything in the MGO Fast Track.
✓ SELECT
Level 2 — ability to buy calls and puts. Do not apply for Level 3 or higher. You are not there yet and it can hurt your approval.
Level 1 — Covered calls only Level 2 — Buy calls & puts ← This is the goal Level 3+ — Selling options (not for beginners)

What is paper trading?
Paper trading is simulated trading with virtual money on a real platform. The charts are real, the prices are real, the order mechanics are real — but no actual money is at risk. This is where you apply every lesson from the Fast Track and build confidence before anything else.
